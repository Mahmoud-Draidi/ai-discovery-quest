A practical course for using real AI tools. Follow Understand → Try it → Check, inspect actual results, and keep private notes. No knowledge of the authors is needed.

This guide is generated from course data with `node scripts/generate-course-guide.cjs`. Do not maintain a separate chapter count or duplicate lesson list.

## Honest progress

The app records your report; it cannot observe external chats or files. Only chapters with all practical checks and knowledge questions complete earn 100 XP. Level titles reflect the percentage of current chapters completed; viewing pages, repeating answers, skipping steps and recording prior experience do not earn XP. If tools are missing, defer the individual step and explore another. Deferred/skipped steps stay unfinished; completion requires every practical check. Existing experience is optional and separate from checked practice.

## Study support

Use the compact companion from Try it to keep the current step visible beside your AI tool. The companion can stay on top and synchronizes its checkbox with the main lesson; only the main app saves progress. Use Next step to explore without claiming completion, My chapter note to return to the main note editor, or Return to lesson. Practice-step buttons let you revisit unfinished work directly. Use chapter questions/sources, library search and topic filters, and your notes when returning.

## Capstone

Extend the same tiny tracker through planning, a worktree, bounded delegation, a pull request and review, and an inspected recurring check. This is different from developing or releasing this learning app.

## Product availability

Inspect the mode, account, tool and host before each external task. Labels and capabilities change. Product comparisons are reference unless you actually try them; extra competitor subscriptions are optional. Never paste credentials into lesson notes or chat.
