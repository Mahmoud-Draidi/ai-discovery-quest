# Changelog

## 0.2.2 — 26 October 2026

- Removes fixed course-size claims from the app and public documentation.
- Derives progress and quiz totals from course data.
- Uses the latest-release download page so links stay current.

## 0.2.1 — 26 October 2026

- Expands the Windows app to standalone AI learning chapters, quiz questions, and searchable reference entries.
- Adds practical steps, per-chapter notes and milestones, bookmarks, resume points, and optional follow-up tasks.
- Adds local existing-experience records and backup/import with preview and a recovery copy.
- Preserves earlier chapter-one progress; personal data is not bundled in the public app.
- Presents lessons directly to new learners without requiring knowledge of the authors' conversations.
- Adds course content/progress/storage checks and Windows/Linux CI coverage.

Distribution: unsigned Windows x64 ZIP, not an installer or automatic updater. Some exercises require external tools/accounts. No built-in AI coach/API service is included.

## 0.1.0-preview.1 — 25 October 2026

- First Windows preview with the Models, tools, and skills chapter.
- Practical note exercise, knowledge check, local notes/progress, and the AI Explorer milestone.
